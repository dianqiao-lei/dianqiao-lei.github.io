    Thinking Inertia: LLMs Keep Thinking When Told Not To

    Dianqiao Lei, Kevin Qinghong Lin, Pan Lu, Philip Torr, James Zou

    NeurIPS 2026

    We uncover “Thinking Inertia”: LLMs continue to produce explicit reasoning despite strict no-thinking controls. Our evaluation across six models shows that this persistence grows as answer spaces become more open, with open-ended tasks revealing a trade-off between answer-only compliance and accuracy. These findings highlight no-thinking as a capability to evaluate alongside reasoning ability.
